# Stock Analysis Reference Notes

When evaluating a stock, combine three inputs:

1. Price action for short-term momentum and volatility clues.
2. News flow for catalysts, sentiment, and management or regulatory updates.
3. Basic company context such as product cycle, earnings timing, and macro sensitivity.

Good agent answers should separate observation from conclusion. Observations describe what the tools returned. Conclusions explain why those signals matter for the user's question.

Risk-aware analysis should mention uncertainty, especially when the data source is delayed, mocked, or incomplete.
